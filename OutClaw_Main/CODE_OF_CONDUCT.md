# Code of Conduct

OutClaw contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to help keep project
spaces welcoming, practical, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct, including during technical disagreement.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, and decisions—not people.
- Assume good faith while still asking for evidence and reproducible behavior.
- Protect private case information and never pressure anyone to disclose it.
- Make room for different levels of technical and legal-domain experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, retaliation, sexualized abuse,
publication of private information, credential sharing, and deliberate exposure
of case evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the hosting
provider's private channel when available. Maintainers may remove content,
restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Security and privacy
reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
