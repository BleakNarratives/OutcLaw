
FILING INSTRUCTIONS FOR KANSAS

1. REVIEW THE COMPLAINT
   - Read the entire complaint carefully
   - Verify all information is accurate
   - Add any additional details you wish to include

2. SIGN THE COMPLAINT
   - Sign and date the complaint where indicated
   - Your signature certifies the complaint is truthful

3. GATHER ATTACHMENTS
   - All evidence documents are in the grievance package folder
   - Include all attachments listed in the complaint

4. FILE WITH BAR ASSOCIATION
   Contact: Kansas Office of the Disciplinary Administrator
   Address: 700 SW Jackson St., Suite 650, Topeka, KS 66603
   Phone: (785) 296-2486
   Website: https://www.kscourts.org/KSCourts/media/KsCourts/Disciplinary%20Administrator/Complaint-Form.pdf
   Online Filing: https://www.kscourts.org/about-the-courts/disciplinary-administrator/file-a-complaint/

5. FILING OPTIONS
   - Online: Visit the complaint URL above
   - Mail: Send to the address above
   - In Person: Visit the bar association office

6. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
   - Bar association will acknowledge receipt (usually within 2 weeks)
   - Investigation will be opened
   - Attorney will be notified and must respond
   - Attorney must notify their malpractice insurance carrier
   - You may be contacted for additional information

7. IMPORTANT NOTES
   - Filing is FREE (no filing fee)
   - You do not need an attorney to file
   - The bar association handles the investigation
   - Attorney must respond within 20 days (typically)
   - Attorney's insurance premiums will increase

8. TRACKING YOUR GRIEVANCE
   - Keep your grievance ID: Will be assigned by bar
   - Bar will provide case number upon receipt
   - You can check status online or by phone

YOUR COST TO FILE: $0.00

This is Code Judo. You spend nothing. They spend everything.
